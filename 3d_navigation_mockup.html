Gabay navigation, in 3D

The screenshot's layout on your penthouse model: search, floor chip, zoom and recentre, an ETA sheet and four tabs. The route is computed live from the real walkway graph in penthouse.json. This is a mock-up, not Flutter code.

Try it

  • Drag the map to pan. Use + / − and the arrow to zoom and recentre. The cube button flips 3D and flat.
  • Tap a purple pin, a search result or a Places row for the place card, then Go.
  • Tap the sheet's chevron for the full route panel. Turn on Wheelchair route and pick Restroom (west) to see the "no wheelchair route" message.
  • Me switches role, Simple/Technical wording and light/dark. Test tools (tester and admin only) turns on beacons, stickers, doors and the walkway graph.
